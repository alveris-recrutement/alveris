Executive Search — France

Hiring Sales Directors,
Country Managers & Managing Directors in France

Alveris helps international groups build or strengthen their commercial and operational leadership in France, through direct, confidential approach.

An international group entering or growing in France often needs one of three related profiles: a Country Manager, a Sales Director, or a Managing Director for a French subsidiary — close in scope, but distinct in responsibility.

Alveris helps international industrial and commercial groups recruit the leadership needed to represent, sell or operate in France: local legal representation, P&L ownership, or a purely commercial mandate, depending on the structure you already have in place. As with every Alveris search, candidates are identified by name and approached directly and in confidence — not sourced from public applications.

This is often the first hire a group makes when formalizing its presence in France, and the quality of that first leader shapes how the market reads the group's commitment to the country. Getting it right matters more than for a typical mid-market hire.

Three related roles

Country Manager, Sales Director or Managing Director?

A Country Manager typically holds legal representation for the group in France and owns the local P&L within a broader regional or global structure — the role fits groups that want a single accountable leader without yet standing up a full local subsidiary. A Managing Director of a French subsidiary carries fuller local responsibility, usually with a more established local structure reporting to it. A Sales Director for France is narrower and purely commercial: building and running the local sales organization without P&L or legal representation responsibilities. Getting the distinction right at the briefing stage avoids recruiting the wrong seniority or scope for the role.

Our approach

Direct approach, scoped to commercial leadership.

These profiles are rarely in active job search — the best candidates are already leading a comparable operation for a competitor or an adjacent group. Alveris identifies them by name, approaches them individually and in confidence, and evaluates their fit against your specific structure (legal representation needed or not, P&L scope, reporting line) before any presentation. For international clients, the scoping call and candidate presentations are conducted in English, with a single point of contact managing the mandate end to end.

Questions

Hiring leadership in France.

What is the difference between a Country Manager and a Managing Director in France?
A Country Manager typically represents the group legally in France and owns the local P&L within a broader regional or global structure. A Managing Director of a French subsidiary carries full local responsibility, often reporting directly to group headquarters.
Do you also recruit purely commercial roles, not just country leadership?
Yes. A Sales Director for France is a narrower, commercially-focused mandate than a Country Manager or Managing Director role, and we approach it with the same direct-approach method, scoped to sales leadership rather than full P&L ownership.
What is the typical timeline for this type of search?
At Alveris, the first qualified candidates are typically presented within about 10 days of the briefing, with a full mandate usually completed in 6 to 10 weeks.
